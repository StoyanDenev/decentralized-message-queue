Findings:

**A1 — HIGH, CONFIRMED. The rule is asserted at the config-load layer over a variable no accept rule reads.** `m_creators` reaches the validator only as `set_m_pool` → `m_pool_` (`include/determ/node/validator.hpp:31,423`), which nothing reads; it reaches the producer as `m_pool_size` and hits `(void)m_pool_size;` (`src/node/producer.cpp:1254`); in `node.cpp` it survives as a log string (`:658-665`), an RPC preview (`:3428-3431`) and the `build_body` argument. DECISION-LOG `ddfe877` (2026-08-14 heading, committed 08-16) records exactly this. It is the doctrine's own case study: GB-8 is 16/16 with two mutants confirmed RED and proves nothing about attributability.
